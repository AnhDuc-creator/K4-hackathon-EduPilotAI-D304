# Log khao sat nguoi that (Evidence duong A)

Hinh thuc: Google Form, gui cho hoc vien khoa AI Thuc Chien ngoai nhom.
So nguoi tra loi: **28** (mot cau co 27 luot vi khong bat buoc).
Anh chup toan bo bang tong hop dat trong `evidence/anh-khao-sat/`.

## Cau 1. Trong buoi hoc gan nhat, ban da gap kho khan o giai doan nao?

n = 27

| Lua chon | Ty le |
|---|---|
| Tiep can kien thuc moi (can giai thich khai niem) | **55,6%** |
| Tong hop va on tap (can he thong hoa noi dung) | 33,3% |
| Thuc hanh bai tap (can huong dan giai tung buoc) | ~7,4% |
| Danh gia nang luc (can phan hoi ve ket qua lam bai) | ~3,7% |

Y nghia: khoanh khac vuong nhieu nhat dung la luc hoc vien tiep can khai niem moi, tuc dung
khoanh khac ma nhom chon lam lat cat.

## Cau 2. Tai khoanh khac do, hanh dong nao cua AI Tutor khien ban chua hai long?

Cau nhieu lua chon, n = 28.

| Lua chon | So nguoi | Ty le |
|---|---|---|
| **Giai thich qua chung chung, khong di vao trong tam van de** | **20** | **71,4%** |
| Khong hieu ngu canh cua cau hoi truoc do | 15 | 53,6% |
| Phan hoi qua cham hoac xuat hien khong dung luc | 11 | 39,3% |
| Khong dinh huong duoc toi can hoc tiep cai gi | 11 | 39,3% |
| Dua ra dap an truc tiep thay vi goi y de toi tu tu duy | 8 | 28,6% |

**Day la con so xac nhan cua duong A: 20/28 = 71,4% vuot nguong 50%.**

Y nghia: dung mot nuoc di giang lai cho moi cau hoi thi ra dung cam giac "giai thich chung chung,
khong vao trong tam" ma 20 nguoi neu.

## Cau 3. Neu co the thay doi mot nang luc cua AI Tutor, ban muon no lam gi?

n = 28

| Lua chon | Ty le |
|---|---|
| Dat cau hoi nguoc lai de dan dat tu duy cua toi | **28,6%** |
| Tom tat lai nhung gi toi da hieu va chua hieu | 25,0% |
| Chia nho van de thanh cac buoc nho hon de toi de hieu | 21,4% |
| Cung cap vi du thuc te lien quan truc tiep den van de cua toi | 17,9% |
| Ket noi kien thuc hien tai voi cac bai hoc truoc do | ~7,1% |

Y nghia: hai lua chon dan dau (dat cau hoi nguoc lai, cung cap vi du thuc te) cong lai 46,5%,
va ca hai deu la nuoc di ma san pham nay sinh ra: `goi_mo` va `cho_vi_du`.

## Cau 4. Muc do hai long ve "dung luc" va "dung ngu canh"

Thang Likert 5 muc, ba menh de. Menh de "AI Tutor hieu ro chu de toi dang hoc" tap trung o
Dong y, con "AI Tutor xuat hien khi toi thuc su can" phan tan ve phia khong dong y.

Y nghia: van de khong nam o cho AI hieu sai chu de, ma nam o cho AI phan ung khong dung kieu.

## Cau 5. Mo ta ngan gon mot lan ban da hoi nhung AI Tutor khong giup ban hieu ra van de

2 cau tra loi tu luan, nguyen van:

1. "AI Tutor khong nho prompt truoc do cua toi"
2. "Toi muon tom tat slide nhung AI khong the lam duoc"

## Cau 6. Buoc tien nao sau day se giup ban hoc hieu qua hon?

n = 28

| Lua chon | Ty le |
|---|---|
| AI tu dong nhan dien lo hong kien thuc va goi y lo trinh ngay khi toi bi ket | 42,9% |
| **AI chi dong vai tro huong dan quy trinh tu duy thay vi dua ra loi giai** | **32,1%** |
| AI cung cap cac bai tap bo tro dua tren chinh sai lam toi vua mac phai | 25,0% |

## Ket luan cua duong A

Dat chuan: n = 28 (>= 20) va 20/28 = 71,4% (>= 50%) xac nhan gap dung van de nhom chon.

## Han che phai ghi ro

- Cau 2 la cau nhieu lua chon nen mot nguoi co the tick nhieu o. Con so 71,4% nghia la
  71,4% nguoi tra loi CO chon muc do, khong phai 71,4% coi do la van de lon nhat.
- Cau 5 chi co 2 nguoi tra loi tu luan, khong du de suy rong.
- Nguoi tra loi la hoc vien cung khoa, khong phai mau ngau nhien.
